Featured pieces: several works from the archive shown together as one article, under a title of their own.

Each file is named YYYY-MM-DD-slug.txt (the date it was featured; the newest opens the site). The first line is
the title; after a blank line, list the works in order, one per line, by their slug (a work's file name without
its date and .txt, e.g. "the-chameleon"). Any kind of work can go in: poems, prose, a Bandcamp track, and so on.

Featured pieces appear in the flow (the < and > on Hurricane 2.0), never in the Work list.
