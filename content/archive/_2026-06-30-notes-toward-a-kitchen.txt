Notes Toward a Kitchen
author: Max Rothman
link: https://macksrothman.com
<subtitle>placeholder text, with verse inside prose</subtitle>

Every kitchen is a theory of the day. Where the knives hang says how much you expect to cut; where the light falls says when you expect to be there. Mine faces west, which means it is a kitchen for evenings, and for the kind of cooking that takes its time.

I keep a few lines taped above the stove, copied out years ago from a notebook whose owner I no longer remember:

<poem>
Salt first, then patience,
    then whatever the market had.
</poem>
which is as good a recipe as I know, and covers most of what I make.
