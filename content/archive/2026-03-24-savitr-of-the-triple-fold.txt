Savitr of the Triple Fold
author: Desiring Machines
link: https://desiringmachines.com

Bright leaves gleam hauntgreen
Bright as second sun.
Astonished, she scrutinizes the third,
The übermenschy,
On oblivious palm.

The way up and the way down
Are one and the same,
In the going.

She lingers in suspension.
Tonight, rolling in her welter,
Licking the one, recurrent wound,
Will she curse a human god?
