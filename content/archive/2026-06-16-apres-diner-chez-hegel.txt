Après dîner chez Hegel
author: John Coyle
link: https://desiringmachines.com

|**I**
What were man but in a darkness of the mind?
And what were this full red fruit without
This porcelain bowl of bluest white and clearest blue to bear it?
And what spirit, holy or otherwise, goes, peregrine, between?

|**II**
Consider then this long and noble face, baked
By sun and wizened by the wisdom of the plains.
See it, as you will, tipped by a long feather white and black.
Consider, if you can, its singular singularity
Its place in rank among the mighty sons of Bharat
Its christening by an ingenioso son of something.

|**III**
She is a girl of infinite affections. He, the burning parvenu,
Posits her a whole, her madness and her chastity.
Now at last it can commence, the wild rural rondelay
With the girl, wild with ribbons in her long and wild hair.
