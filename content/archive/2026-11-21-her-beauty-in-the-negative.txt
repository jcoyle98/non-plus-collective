her beauty in the negative
author: Mephisto

the tears of her eyes
compare not to bodies
as filthy and disgusting as the ocean,
rife with rot and decay,
shapeless, writhing. 

nor barren as the Moon
she stands at attention
‘neath uninspired Sun,
herself burning.

nor odorless as a Rose 
festering thorns,
the fetid feminine
whom fortune fated not.
