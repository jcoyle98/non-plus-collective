‘Spider’ (1996) in my Bedroom
author: Brooke Corso
link: https://brookecorso.com

I wake each morning to a gossamer weather above my head
Silken filaments streaming across the ceiling,

I’m like an infant in a trance, mesmerized by a mobile made of delicate
Tendrils dangling off the chandelier, undulating in the breeze

*I forgot to close the window again*
*What if it escaped?*

I tug at a memory: third grade, cross legged on a rough carpet
Shouting *cumulonimbus!* at a laminated image pinned on the wall

*Cumulonimbus* – no, that’s not what these are
*Stratus?* I’m not sure about that either

I used to be afraid of spiders, then: Louise Bourgeois in Berlin, sometime in 2022
My best friend explaining that *her mother was a weaver, that’s why she made spiders*

*Acceptance*
They’re just little looms

When I first saw it abseil down a pearl thread, dangling in the wide open
I wanted to swat: crush it between magazine and drywall but

Now each morning I look forward
to seeing the tiny sky appear
