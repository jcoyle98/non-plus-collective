Vespers
author: Mary Kate Tankard
link: https://marykatetankard.com
<subtitle>placeholder text, with numbered parts</subtitle>

|**I**
The bell is late tonight, or I am early.
The light goes amber on the kitchen tiles
and stays there, as if it had been asked to.

|**II**
I keep the hour the way my mother kept it:
not praying, exactly, but standing still
long enough for something to arrive.

|**III**
It does not always arrive.
    That is part of it.
