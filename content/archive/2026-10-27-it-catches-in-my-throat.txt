it catches in my throat
author: Brooke Corso
link: https://brookecorso.com

I strike a match against the box but
It won’t catch
So I strike again,
And again, and again, and again

Disgruntled in the dull glow of the nightlight
Steam swells around me like smoke, water gushing out of the faucet and filling the tub

I discard the charred matchsticks in the bin
One on top of another on top of another
On top of tissue, on top of floss, on top of used q-tips

I’ve wasted so many.
Accumulation is the evidence of my failure

I give up without
Ever lighting the candle
No wax pool forming, no wish when I blow out the flame
What would I even wish for?

I have one of your lighters, still, the one with the watermelon print
From a night last fall when we smoked on my front steps
Sitting close, knees pulled in, barely touching – but touching

One cigarette after another, after another
Sick to my stomach from all the nicotine but agreeing to one more because
*Sure, why not*

So we pass another back and forth and back and forth,
Your lips, my lips
Yours again, mine again

You say hi to everyone who passes by
I don’t even notice them

I want to let it out, right then, right there
The words in my mind that have caught like a spark in dry brush
All consuming, engulfing me, choking me in the fumes

Every time I try to say it,
It catches in my throat

Imagining everything we’ve so precariously propped up between us suddenly ablaze
And me, left sifting ashes through my fingers
Without an urn on the mantle to mourn

So I keep the words in,
And now an arroyo winds through my mind
Barren curves, deserted twists, winding on and on and on

I feel the words in my throat, still, while trailing down the hallway back to my bedroom
Avoiding the creaks in the floorboards

I know exactly where your lighter is:
Left nightstand, in the back, mixed in with the palo santo and the dead weed pens
I turn the lighter over in my palm, imagining it in your hand, or in your pocket, or in your room

Back in the bathroom, the matchsticks taunt me from the trash
How many times I tried unsuccessfully to strike a match
How many times I tried unsuccessfully to tell you how I feel

So I leave your lighter on the counter, atop my folded pajamas, beside the unlit candle
And I sink into the bath
