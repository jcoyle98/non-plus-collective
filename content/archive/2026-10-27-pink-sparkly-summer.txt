pink sparkly summer
author: Brooke Corso
link: https://brookecorso.com

i.
Sinking my teeth in, grinding them against the pit, pulling back with
Nectarine juice dripping down onto me and
Splattering on the sun-hot leather seat between my legs
Speeding, wind whipping through the open sunroof, sand still caked up my calves
One hand on the wheel, the other in your mouth
Each finger a perfect popsicle of tangy sweet and sweat, just for you
*Me and Michael, solid as they come* twinkles through the speakers
Sparkly pink and orange striped sky spread out over us like a patchwork quilt –
Like the kind you’d dig up from a moth ball filled trunk at the cabin in the summertime
Every red light - stop - then, your tongue in my mouth *- don’t stop* - slimly, sticky, I want it again

ii.
Then, at the bar, ruby-red bulbs stream over our heads like chemtrails
Clandestine energy conjured all around us, radiating like a force field
We’re little kids huddled over potions on a rickety table in the back corner
Usurping the music, I insist on *Strange Magic*
And while I sing along, you knock the glass over, plum wine sinking into the cherry pine
Right at home on the sticky floor,
An accidental hex
Laughter exorcized out of my body, your hand cradling my scalp as you pull me in
Teeth clash, you’re laughing too, we kiss through it, spellbound

iii.
Violet light biome of your apartment
I stand dripping in the hallway, vespertine
Pilling white towel hanging off my shoulders like angel wings
Hairs on the back of my neck attentive, devotional in the breeze from the window
Jammed open by the book I bought for you, my confession etched inside the first page
You pat the spot beside you on the couch, embossing an indent where
I curl up, halo in your lap, and we pass the joint back and forth, silently
The smoke you exhale tumbling down on me like wisteria
My skin luminescent in the twilight, yours melting into the mauve
I’m restless; biting at your leg, drawing blood like a pomegranate broken open
Scarlett everywhere
