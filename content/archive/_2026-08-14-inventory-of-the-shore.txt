Inventory of the Shore
author: Brooke Corso
link: https://brookecorso.com

One gull, undecided.
    A rope, frayed to a brush.
        Glass, softened by the sea into something like an apology.
A child's shoe, the left one,
    which is always the one the water keeps.

| ~

What the tide brings in it counts again by morning,
and the count is never the same,
    and nobody minds.
